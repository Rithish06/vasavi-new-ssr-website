What CGHS is

The Central Government Health Scheme is run by the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India. It provides healthcare to serving central government employees, pensioners and their eligible dependants.

Beneficiaries hold a CGHS card and receive primary care at CGHS Wellness Centres. For specialist consultations, diagnostics and surgical treatment they are referred to empanelled hospitals, where care is provided in line with the scheme's notified guidelines and procedures.

Eligibility and card status are confirmed by your CGHS Wellness Centre, which remains the correct authority for those questions.

  • Who it covers Serving central government employees, pensioners, family pensioners and their eligible dependants.
  • How access works A valid CGHS card, plus a referral from your Wellness Centre for planned specialist care.
  • What it covers Specialist consultations, diagnostics, day-care procedures, surgery and inpatient care.
  • Who sets the rules CGHS, not the hospital - including eligibility, referrals and scheme rates.
