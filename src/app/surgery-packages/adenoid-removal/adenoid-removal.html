What is Adenoid Removal?

Adenoids are a small pad of tissue located high up behind the nose, above the tonsils. They're most active in early childhood and normally shrink by the teenage years - but when they become enlarged or repeatedly infected, they can block the airway and eustachian tubes.

If your child snores loudly, breathes through the mouth, or gets frequent ear or sinus infections, don't ignore the signs. Vasavi Hospitals offers safe, painless adenoidectomy in Bangalore - a quick day-care procedure with no external cuts and a fast return to normal life.

What Happens If Enlarged Adenoids Are Left Untreated?

Most parents don't realise how much untreated adenoids are quietly affecting their child - here's what tends to happen when it's left unaddressed, year after year.

Mouth Breathing & Facial Growth

Long-term mouth breathing in growing children is a recognised contributor to changes in facial and dental development over time.

Disrupted Sleep & Daytime Behaviour

Untreated sleep apnea in children has been linked to poor concentration, behavioural issues, and daytime tiredness that's often mistaken for something else.

Recurrent Ear Infections & Hearing Impact

Blocked eustachian tubes can lead to persistent fluid buildup ("glue ear") - temporary hearing loss that can quietly affect speech and learning if it drags on.

Recurring Infections & Missed School Days

Repeated sinus and respiratory infections mean repeated antibiotics and missed school days - without fixing the underlying blockage causing them.
