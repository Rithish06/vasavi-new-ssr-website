What is ACL Reconstruction?

ACL Reconstruction is a surgical procedure to replace a torn Anterior Cruciate Ligament (ACL) - one of the main ligaments that stabilise the knee. It is commonly needed after sports injuries, road accidents or sudden twisting movements that leave the knee painful, swollen or unstable.

At Vasavi Hospitals, our orthopaedic surgeons perform Arthroscopic ACL Reconstruction - a minimally invasive keyhole procedure where the torn ligament is replaced with a tendon graft through tiny incisions, for faster healing, minimal scarring and a stronger, stable knee.

 Watch out for these signs

A "Pop" Sound at the Moment of Injury Swelling Within a Few Hours Knee "Giving Way" or Slipping Pain While Pivoting or on Stairs Difficulty Bearing Weight

Why Ignoring an ACL Tear Is Risky

An ACL tear won't rupture overnight like an appendix - but every "giving way" episode quietly damages the rest of the knee. Here's what tends to happen when instability is left untreated.

Meniscus Tears

An unstable knee shifts abnormally with every twist - and the meniscus (the knee's shock absorber) often pays the price, tearing in later giving-way episodes.

Cartilage Damage & Early Arthritis

Long-standing instability grinds down joint cartilage, and untreated ACL injuries are strongly linked to early-onset knee osteoarthritis.

Chronic Instability

The knee buckles on stairs, slopes and uneven ground - turning everyday movements into fall risks and forcing you to give up sports you love.

Muscle Weakness & Harder Rehab

The longer the knee is guarded and underused, the more the thigh muscles waste - making eventual surgery and rehabilitation slower and tougher.

A single evaluation with an MRI can tell you clearly how stable your knee is and what to do next.

What to Expect During ACL Reconstruction Surgery

Your Step-by-Step Care Journey

1
Consultation & Evaluation

Meet our orthopaedic team for a detailed knee examination and imaging (MRI / X-ray). We explain the extent of ligament damage, your treatment options, cost and the full recovery plan before anything is decided.

2
Surgery Day

The procedure is done under spinal or general anaesthesia using arthroscopic (keyhole) techniques. The torn ACL is reconstructed with a tendon graft, positioned precisely for stability. It usually takes 1-2 hours.

3
Post-Surgery Stay

You'll stay in hospital for 1-2 days. Our physiotherapists start gentle knee movement almost immediately to prevent stiffness, and you'll go home with a clear week-by-week rehab plan.

4
Recovery & Rehabilitation

Most patients walk with support within days and return to desk work in 1-2 weeks. Jogging typically resumes around 3 months, and return to pivoting sports takes 6-9 months - guided by physiotherapy strength milestones.

Have questions about your surgery day or rehab plan? We're here to walk you through every step.
