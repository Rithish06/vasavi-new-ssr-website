What is Piles (Haemorrhoids) Surgery?

Piles, or haemorrhoids, are swollen blood vessels in and around the anus that cause pain, itching, and bleeding, especially during bowel movements. They are extremely common - often caused by straining, prolonged sitting, low-fibre diets, pregnancy, or chronic constipation - and range from mild, manageable swellings to prolapsed lumps that need surgery.

At Vasavi Hospitals, our surgeons offer both Laser Haemorrhoidectomy - a minimally invasive, precise procedure with minimal bleeding and faster recovery - and Conventional (Open) Haemorrhoidectomy, a proven, time-tested technique for more advanced cases, so every patient gets the approach best suited to their condition.

 Watch out for these signs

Bleeding During Bowel Movements Pain, Itching or Irritation A Lump Near the Anus Discomfort While Sitting A Feeling of Incomplete Bowel Emptying

Why Ignoring Piles Is Risky

Piles won't turn into an emergency overnight like an appendix - but every month of delay lets the condition progress further. Here's what tends to happen when piles are left untreated.

Chronic Bleeding & Anaemia

Repeated, untreated bleeding can add up over months, sometimes leading to iron-deficiency anaemia and fatigue that patients don't connect back to piles.

Worsening Grade

Mild, internal piles that once went unnoticed can progress to prolapsed lumps that no longer go back in on their own, needing more involved treatment.

Thrombosed (Clotted) Piles

A neglected pile can suddenly clot, causing severe, sharp pain that often lands patients in emergency care rather than a planned, comfortable procedure.

Recurring Pain & Discomfort

Untreated piles tend to flare up again and again - disrupting sitting, sleep and daily life, and often eroding confidence about the condition getting better on its own.

A single, discreet evaluation can tell you clearly what grade your piles are and what to do next.

What to Expect During Piles Surgery

Your Step-by-Step Care Journey

1
Consultation & Grading

Our surgeons perform a discreet, comfortable examination to grade your piles and explain the extent of the condition, your treatment options, and the full recovery plan before anything is decided.

2
Procedure Day

Under anaesthesia, the surgeon performs either Laser or Conventional Haemorrhoidectomy, chosen to suit your grade and condition. The procedure typically takes 30-60 minutes.

3
Day-Care / Short Hospital Stay

Many laser patients go home the same day or after one night; conventional surgery patients may stay slightly longer. Pain management and clear aftercare instructions are provided before discharge.

4
Recovery & Follow-Up

Most patients resume light activity within a few days and desk work within a week to 10 days. Your surgeon will guide you on diet and hygiene to support healing and reduce the chance of recurrence.
