What is Ovarian Cystectomy?

Ovarian Cystectomy is a surgical procedure to remove cysts from one or both ovaries while preserving healthy ovarian tissue. These cysts are usually fluid-filled sacs that can cause pain, bloating or irregular periods.

At Vasavi Hospitals, our skilled gynecologists perform Laparoscopic Ovarian Cystectomy, a minimally invasive surgery that involves small incisions and advanced instruments for precise removal. This ensures less pain, minimal scarring and a faster recovery compared to open surgery.

If you experience pelvic pain, bloating or irregular menstrual cycles, consult our specialists for an accurate diagnosis and the best treatment plan.

Know the Signs. Choose the Right Care.

Ovarian cysts are common, but some may grow large or become painful if left untreated. At Vasavi Hospitals, we detect the condition early and treat it effectively using advanced laparoscopic surgery to restore comfort and hormonal balance.

Common Symptoms

  • Lower abdominal or pelvic pain
  • Bloating or a feeling of fullness in the abdomen
  • Irregular menstrual periods
  • Pain during intercourse or urination
  • Nausea or heaviness in the lower belly

Surgery Options

At Vasavi Hospitals, Ovarian Cystectomy is performed using Laparoscopic techniques for better precision and quicker recovery. The cyst is safely removed while preserving the ovary, helping maintain fertility and hormonal health.

What to Expect During Ovarian Cystectomy Surgery

Your Step-by-Step Care Journey

1

Consultation & Evaluation

Meet our gynecology team for a detailed evaluation. We perform ultrasound and other diagnostic tests to assess the cyst’s size and type before planning surgery.

2

Surgery Day

The procedure is performed under general anesthesia using Laparoscopic methods. Small incisions are made in the abdomen to remove the cyst safely while preserving the ovary. The surgery typically takes 1-2 hours.

3

Post-Surgery Stay

You’ll stay in the hospital for 1-2 days for observation. Our doctors provide post-operative guidance on diet, rest and medication to ensure smooth recovery.

4

Recovery & Follow-Up

Most patients resume light activities within 5-7 days. Follow-up visits help monitor healing and confirm the return of normal ovarian function.
