What is Sinus Surgery?

Sinus surgery clears blocked sinus passages so mucus can drain and air can flow normally again - relieving the congestion, pressure, and headaches of chronic sinusitis. It's typically done entirely through the nostrils, with no external incisions.

Chronic sinusitis doesn't just mean a stuffy nose - most people with it report disrupted sleep, and the resulting fatigue and brain fog can affect work and daily life for years if left untreated. Vasavi Hospitals offers Balloon Sinuplasty and FESS in Bangalore to give you lasting relief, not just temporary symptom control.

What Happens If Chronic Sinusitis Is Left Untreated?

Most patients don't realise how much a blocked sinus is quietly costing them - here's what tends to happen when it's managed with medication alone, year after year.

Recurring Infections & Antibiotic Overuse

4+ infections a year, each treated with another round of antibiotics - without fixing the underlying blockage causing them.

Disrupted Sleep & Chronic Fatigue

Most chronic sinusitis patients report poor sleep - the resulting fatigue and brain fog can quietly affect work and daily life for years.

Worsening Nasal Polyps

Left untreated, polyps tend to grow larger over time - making the eventual procedure more involved than if addressed earlier.

Reduced Quality of Life

Constant congestion, reduced sense of smell, and low energy add up - many patients only realise how much once it's gone.

Treatment Options at Vasavi Hospitals

We offer both Balloon Sinuplasty and FESS, performed by 25+ year experienced ENT surgeons. Here's what sets each approach apart.

Minimally Invasive

Balloon Sinuplasty

A small balloon catheter gently widens blocked sinus passages - no tissue removal, minimal trauma. Best suited for less severe, uncomplicated chronic sinusitis.

Minimal tissue trauma
Recovery in just 1–2 days
Can often be done under local anaesthesia
Ideal for uncomplicated cases
Minimal post-op discomfort
Thorough Standard

FESS (Functional Endoscopic Sinus Surgery)

A proven, more comprehensive technique that removes diseased tissue, polyps, and blockages using an endoscope - ideal for complex, recurrent, or polyp-related sinusitis.

Handles nasal polyps & complex cases
More thorough, longer-lasting relief
Recovery in 7–10 days
Suitable after failed prior treatment
Covered under most insurance plans
