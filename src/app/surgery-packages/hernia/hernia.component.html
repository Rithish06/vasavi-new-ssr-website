What is Hernia?

A hernia occurs when an internal organ or tissue pushes through a weak spot in the abdominal wall, often causing a small lump or discomfort. It develops due to strain, heavy lifting, or long-term pressure on the abdomen.

If you notice a bulge near your abdomen or groin, or feel pain while coughing or lifting, don't ignore the signs. Vasavi Hospitals offers affordable hernia surgery in Bangalore - with expert diagnosis, advanced robotic treatment, and a care team that guides you every step of the way.

 Watch out for these signs

Visible Bulge Groin or Abdominal Pain Pain While Lifting Burning Sensation Heaviness After Standing

Why Delaying Hernia Surgery Is Risky

A hernia does not heal on its own - it only gets larger with time. Here's what tends to happen the longer it's left untreated.

Risk of Strangulation

If trapped tissue loses its blood supply, it becomes a life-threatening emergency requiring urgent surgery - the single biggest reason not to delay.

The Hernia Only Grows Larger

Without surgery, the weak spot keeps widening over time - what starts as a small, easily-repaired bulge can become a larger, more complex one.

A More Complex, Costlier Repair Later

Larger, longstanding hernias are technically harder to repair and typically mean a longer surgery and recovery than treating it early.

Ongoing Pain Limiting Daily Life

Discomfort while lifting, standing, or exercising tends to persist and worsen - many patients quietly restrict their activity for years rather than treat it.

A single evaluation can tell you clearly how urgent your case is and what to do next.
