What is Gallbladder Stone Removal Surgery?

Gallbladder removal, also known as Cholecystectomy, is a surgical procedure performed to remove the gallbladder when it contains stones or causes pain, infection or digestive problems. Gallstones are hardened deposits that block bile flow, leading to severe abdominal discomfort, nausea and inflammation.

If you notice sharp pain in the upper right abdomen, nausea after meals, or indigestion, don't ignore the signs. Vasavi Hospitals offers affordable gallbladder surgery in Bangalore using both Laparoscopic and Robotic techniques - with expert diagnosis and a care team that guides you every step of the way.

 Watch out for these signs

Sharp Upper Right Abdomen Pain Nausea After Fatty Meals Indigestion or Bloating Fever or Rib Discomfort Pain After Eating

Why Delaying Gallbladder Surgery Is Risky

Gallstones do not dissolve on their own - once symptoms start, delaying treatment raises the risk of complications. Here's what tends to happen the longer it's left untreated.

Acute Cholecystitis & Infection

A blocked gallbladder duct can become severely inflamed and infected, requiring urgent surgery rather than a planned one.

Gallbladder Rupture or Perforation

Untreated inflammation can cause the gallbladder wall to rupture, spreading infection into the abdomen - a surgical emergency.

Bile Duct Blockage & Jaundice

A stone that slips into the bile duct can block bile flow, causing jaundice and requiring more complex, urgent intervention.

Risk of Pancreatitis

Gallstones are a leading cause of pancreatitis - a painful, potentially serious inflammation of the pancreas that needs hospital care.

A single evaluation can tell you clearly how urgent your case is and what to do next.

What to Expect During Gallbladder Stone Removal Surgery

Your Step-by-Step Care Journey

1
Consultation & Evaluation

Our surgical team will assess your symptoms, perform scans like ultrasound or CT and confirm the presence of gallstones. We explain the procedure, expected recovery and cost clearly before surgery.

2
Surgery Day

The procedure is done under general anaesthesia using Laparoscopic or Robotic-assisted methods. Small incisions are made to remove the gallbladder safely, usually taking about 1 hour.

3
Post-Surgery Stay

You'll stay in the hospital for 1-2 days under observation. Our team monitors your recovery and provides diet guidance and care instructions to avoid post-surgery discomfort.

4
Recovery & Follow-Up

Most patients resume normal activities within 5-7 days. Follow-up visits ensure proper healing, dietary balance and digestive comfort after surgery.
