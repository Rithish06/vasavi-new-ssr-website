What is Tonsillectomy?

Tonsillectomy is the surgical removal of the tonsils - two oval-shaped pads of tissue at the back of the throat that can become repeatedly infected or enlarged. It's one of the most common ENT surgeries performed on both children and adults.

If you or your child suffer from frequent throat infections, loud snoring, or breathing difficulty during sleep, don't ignore the signs. Vasavi Hospitals offers modern coblation tonsillectomy in Bangalore - a gentler technique with less pain and faster healing than traditional surgery.

 Watch out for these signs

Frequent Sore Throat Loud Snoring / Sleep Apnea Difficulty Swallowing Recurrent Tonsil Infections Chronic Bad Breath

What Happens If Recurring Tonsillitis Is Left Untreated?

Most families don't realise how much recurring throat infections are quietly costing them - here's what tends to happen when it's managed with medication alone, year after year.

Recurring Infections & Missed School or Work

Repeated throat infections mean repeated antibiotics, missed school days, and missed workdays - without ever fixing the tonsils causing them.

Disrupted Sleep for the Whole Family

Loud snoring and pauses in breathing don't just affect the patient - untreated sleep apnea in children can also affect growth, concentration, and behaviour over time.

Risk of Peritonsillar Abscess

Recurrent tonsillitis can occasionally progress to a painful, pus-filled abscess needing urgent drainage - a risk that drops once the tonsils are removed.

Reduced Quality of Life

Chronic bad breath, difficulty swallowing, and constant throat discomfort add up - many patients only realise how much once it's resolved.

Treatment Options at Vasavi Hospitals

We offer Coblation and Traditional tonsillectomy techniques, performed by 25+ year experienced ENT surgeons. Here's what sets each approach apart.

Recommended · Modern

Coblation Tonsillectomy

Uses a low-temperature plasma field to remove tonsil tissue, protecting the surrounding muscles and blood vessels - resulting in a gentler procedure, especially suited for children.

Significantly less post-operative pain
Faster return to normal eating (3–5 days)
Lower bleeding risk (~0.5% vs ~3%)
Ideal for children & sleep apnea cases
Shorter operative duration
Trusted Standard

Traditional Tonsillectomy

A proven, widely used technique (cold dissection/bipolar) for complete tonsil removal. A well-established, effective option for most patients.

Complete tonsil removal
Lower upfront cost than coblation
Widely studied, long track record
Suitable for most patients
Covered under most insurance plans
