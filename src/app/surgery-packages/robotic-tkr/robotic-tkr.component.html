What is Total Knee Replacement?

Total Knee Replacement (TKR) is a surgical procedure to replace the worn or damaged surfaces of the knee joint with artificial implants, relieving pain and restoring movement. It is most commonly recommended when arthritis or long-standing injury has worn down the joint enough that walking, climbing stairs, or even resting has become painful.

At Vasavi Hospitals, our orthopaedic surgeons offer both Mako Robotic-Assisted Total Knee Replacement - using 3D CT-based planning and a robotic arm for precise implant alignment - and Conventional Total Knee Replacement, a proven, time-tested technique, so every patient gets the approach best suited to their knee.

 Watch out for these signs

Persistent Pain on Walking or Stairs Stiffness or Swelling, Even at Rest Knee Deformity or Bowing Reduced Range of Motion Pain That Disturbs Sleep

Why Ignoring Knee Arthritis Is Risky

A worn-out knee won't fail overnight like an appendix - but every year of delay lets the damage progress further. Here's what tends to happen when advanced knee arthritis is left untreated.

Further Bone & Cartilage Loss

Once cartilage wears down to bone-on-bone contact, the joint keeps degrading - making the eventual surgery more complex and recovery slower.

Knee Deformity

Untreated arthritis often causes the knee to bow inward or outward over time, which can require more extensive correction during surgery.

Loss of Mobility & Fall Risk

A stiff, painful knee makes stairs, uneven ground and standing up from a chair increasingly difficult - raising fall risk and shrinking independence.

Muscle Weakness & Harder Rehab

The longer the knee is guarded and underused, the more the surrounding muscles waste - making eventual surgery and rehabilitation slower and tougher.

A single evaluation with an X-ray can tell you clearly how worn your knee joint is and what to do next.

What to Expect During Total Knee Replacement Surgery

Your Step-by-Step Care Journey

1
Consultation & Pre-Surgery Planning

Our experts evaluate your X-rays, symptoms and medical history. For Mako robotic surgery, a 3D CT scan maps your knee to build a customised surgical plan before anything is decided.

2
Surgery Day

Under anaesthesia, the surgeon performs either Conventional or Mako Robotic-Assisted knee replacement, ensuring precision, alignment and comfort. It usually takes 1-2 hours.

3
Hospital Stay

Most patients walk with support within 24 hours. A short 2-3 day stay includes structured physiotherapy and pain management for a smooth start to recovery.

4
Recovery & Rehabilitation

Gradual physiotherapy improves flexibility and strength over the following weeks. Most patients return to daily activities within 4-6 weeks, with continued improvement over 2-3 months.
