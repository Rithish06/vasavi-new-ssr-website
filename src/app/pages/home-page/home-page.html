ABOUT SREE VASAVI TRUST
A Legacy of Service. A Commitment to Better Lives.
Established in 1951, Sree Vasavi Trust has been dedicated to serving society through meaningful work in healthcare, education, and social welfare.
Driven by the belief that quality healthcare and education should be accessible to everyone, the Trust works to support communities, empower those in need, and create opportunities for a healthier and better future.
